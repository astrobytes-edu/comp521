## Purpose

Project 6 uses machine learning as a scientific modeling task: define a target,
choose and inspect features, evaluate generalization, and explain what the
model can and cannot support.

## Reading sequence

1. [Supervised learning from scratch](01-supervised-learning.md)
2. [Clustering, PCA, and model evaluation](02-unsupervised-learning.md)

The module builds on the course's earlier habits: make assumptions explicit,
test failure modes, and do not mistake a score for a scientific conclusion.
