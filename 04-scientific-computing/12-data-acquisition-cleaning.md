## Why this chapter belongs here

Real datasets arrive with metadata, gaps, quality flags, outliers, and choices
that affect the scientific claim. This chapter will establish a reproducible
workflow for obtaining, documenting, inspecting, and preparing observational
data before model fitting.

## Planned scope

- retrieving and recording the source of a public dataset;
- preserving raw data separately from derived analysis products;
- interpreting quality flags and missing values;
- making cleaning choices explicit and testable;
- using exploratory plots to find structure and failure modes before fitting.
