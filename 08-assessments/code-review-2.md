Choose **JAX or PyTorch**, or a related library in either ecosystem, and teach
us what you learn. This is an open-ended exploration of widely used
**differentiable AI/ML tools**: software that can compute derivatives of its
calculations automatically. The goal is to become familiar with these tools
and how they support machine learning, data science, and scientific computing.

## Preparation: chapters and lectures

Read **Chapters 2 and 3** of *Deep Learning for Astrophysics* and watch the
