# Scientific Background

These pages are concise, visual references for the scientific ideas that recur
across lectures, labs, and projects. They are **not a second required reading
sequence**. A lecture introduces the minimum needed for that day; these cards
give you one stable place to return when you need the vocabulary, model, or
assumption again.

## How to use a concept card

1. Start with the page your lecture or project links.
2. Identify the **observable**: what is actually measured or computed?
3. Identify the **model**: what simplified relationship connects inputs to an
   output?
4. Read the **limits** before treating the result as a statement about nature.
