# Welcome to COMP 521

```{image} assets/comp521-logo.png
:alt: COMP 521 Scientific Computing and Data Science wordmark with a transit curve, scientific data points, and gold stars.
:width: 820px
:align: center
```

> **Build methods. Test their limits. Use them on real data.**

You will implement core algorithms behind scientific software, including
integration, root-finding, ODE solvers, and model fitting. You will also use
and evaluate introductory machine-learning methods, then apply the course
toolkit to real astronomical data from Gaia and Kepler.

By the end, you will not just *use* `scipy.integrate`; you will know what it is
doing, when it will fail you, and how to tell the difference.

:::{note} Course at a glance

**Prerequisites:** MATH 252; MATH 254 or 342A; or equivalent with instructor
permission, or graduate standing.

## How this course is structured

The semester has five two-week projects, two individual code review
presentations, and a four-week solo final project.
Every class meeting combines instruction and hands-on work, so bring your
laptop.

Complete the laptop setup before class **Thu Aug 27** so that you can use Python
and Jupyter in class. The completion-only **Project 1 Repository Check** is due
**Fri Aug 28 at 11:59 pm PT**: accept your Classroom50 assignment, clone the
repository, and push one setup commit. It does not assess Project 1 progress.
The [Schedule](01-course-info/schedule.md) is the authoritative source for all
dates; [Projects](01-course-info/projects.md) explains what you will build and
submit.
