# Welcome to COMP 521

```{image} assets/comp521-logo.png
:alt: COMP 521 Scientific Computing and Data Science wordmark with a transit curve, scientific data points, and gold stars.
:width: 820px
:align: center
```

> **Build methods. Test their limits. Use them on real data.**

You will implement the core algorithms behind scientific software — integrators,
root-finders, ODE solvers, samplers, and classifiers — then apply them to real
astronomical data from Gaia and Kepler.

By the end, you will not just *use* `scipy.integrate`; you will know what it is
doing, when it will fail you, and how to tell the difference.

:::{note} Course at a glance
**Units:** 3
**Prerequisites:** MATH 252; MATH 254 or 342A; or equivalent with instructor
permission, or graduate standing.
**Instructor:** Anna Rosen · alrosen@sdsu.edu
:::

## How this course is structured

The semester has a one-week setup bootcamp, six two-week projects, and a
four-week solo final project. Every class meeting combines instruction and
hands-on work, so bring your laptop.

Project 0 is due **Thu Aug 27**, our second class meeting. It gets your Python
environment, command line, Git, and Classroom50 workflow working before project
work begins. The [Schedule](01-course-info/schedule.md) is the authoritative
source for all dates; [Projects](01-course-info/projects.md) explains what you
will build and submit.
