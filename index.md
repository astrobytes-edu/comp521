# Welcome to COMP 521

```{image} assets/comp521-logo.png
:alt: COMP 521 Scientific Computing and Data Science wordmark with a transit curve, scientific data points, and gold stars.
:width: 820px
:align: center
```

> **Build methods. Test their limits. Use them on real data.**

You will implement the core algorithms behind scientific software, including
integration, root-finding, ODE solvers, model fitting, and classifiers, then
apply them to real astronomical data from Gaia and Kepler.

By the end, you will not just *use* `scipy.integrate`; you will know what it is
doing, when it will fail you, and how to tell the difference.

:::{note} Course at a glance
**Units:** 3
**Prerequisites:** MATH 252; MATH 254 or 342A; or equivalent with instructor
permission, or graduate standing.
**Instructor:** Anna Rosen · alrosen@sdsu.edu
:::

## How this course is structured

The semester has six two-week projects and a four-week solo final project.
Every class meeting combines instruction and hands-on work, so bring your
laptop.

The completion-only **P1 Technical Readiness Check** is due **before class Thu
Aug 27**. You complete it in your actual Project 1 repository to verify access,
notebook execution, and a successful GitHub push. It does not assess P1
progress. The [Schedule](01-course-info/schedule.md) is the authoritative source
for all dates; [Projects](01-course-info/projects.md) explains what you will
build and submit.
