How can imperfect measurements constrain the physical quantities we want to
understand? This block connects scientific questions, models, probability, and
linear algebra to real data.

## Lecture 13 · Measurements, probability, and geometry

[Open the lecture page](lecture-13-measurements.md) ·
[Open the slides](/reveal/lecture-13-measurements.html) ·
[Read the primer](../06-linear-algebra-stats/00-linear-algebra/00d-measurements-probability-and-geometry.md).

Begin with a transit and the light recorded by a detector. Use a forward model,
measurement uncertainty, and the geometry of data to connect a light curve to
physical quantities. The lecture introduces expectations, dependence,
covariance, correlation, the central limit theorem, vectors, and matrices.

The slide deck includes worked details that you can open with the down arrow.
