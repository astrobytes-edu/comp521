The lecture introduces ODEs, state variables, and physical modeling, then
derives exact growth and decay solutions. Finite differences and Taylor
expansions lead to Euler, midpoint RK2, and RK4. Worked examples compare
accuracy, stability, energy, and phase. Richardson extrapolation and
symplectic leapfrog complete the method comparison. A radial pressure-balance
example connects these methods to planetary interiors.

The deck contains 40 main slides and four optional references after the exit
questions. The handout provides the worked explanations for review.
