The lecture connects a noisy scientific measurement to a forward model,
probability, and uncertainty. It introduces dependence and the central limit
theorem, then develops vectors, features, transformations, and projection as
ways to describe and compare data. Dimensionality reduction provides a preview
of the later PCA discussion.
